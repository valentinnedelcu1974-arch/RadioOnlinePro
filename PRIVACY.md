# Radio 43 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026. Applies to iOS version 1.0, bundle com.radioonlinepro.player.

Developer: Iulian Valentin Nedelcu. Privacy/support contact: valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com.

## Data kept on your iPhone

The app stores saved station names, stream URLs and favorites in local iOS app preferences. These records are not sent to a developer-operated backend. You can remove saved stations in the app. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to any backup settings you control through Apple.

## Internet connections

The catalog is bundled with the app. Searching and filtering the catalog happen locally and do not send your search text to a directory. The app no longer connects to Radio Browser.

The app includes one MDK Space Radio channel. Playback connects directly to https://radio.mdkband.com/stream.mp3. To display attribution and check its licensed source, the app requests https://radio.mdkband.com/now-playing.json before playback and about every 20 seconds while active. Those servers receive your IP address and connection details. We do not control or promise the absence of provider logs. Track/source/license records are bundled; the app does not load album artwork or connect to Internet Archive merely to show credits. Opening a source link uses the browser and that site receives normal browser connection data. No VRT audio connections remain.

## Accounts, tracking and permissions

The advertising integration candidate includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 13.12.0 and Google User Messaging Platform 3.1.0. Commercial advertising is disabled in the shipped configuration. It has no account registration, separate analytics SDK such as Firebase Analytics, developer-operated user database, purchases or subscriptions. Google advertising SDK diagnostics and analytics processing are described below. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not record audio. Favorites remain local. The developer does not sell app-user data or use it for advertising tracking.

## Optional advertising test and future commercial advertising

Opening the app does not initialize Google advertising or contact Google for ads while commercial advertising is disabled. In About & privacy, you may explicitly start the Google test banner. This establishes connections to Google and requests consent information. Google may process IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device/app information, technical identifiers, diagnostics and ad interaction data under https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure. Test ads generate no developer income. You can stop the test; this removes the banner and prevents additional requests from this app, but does not erase data already transmitted.

Before requesting ads, the app refreshes UMP consent information and presents Google's form when required. A privacy-options button is available when required by UMP. If consent cannot be verified, no ad request is made. Radio playback is available without starting the advertising test. Requests are marked nonpersonalized, publisher first-party ID is disabled, and the app does not request tracking authorization or access IDFA. Nonpersonalized advertising can still involve personal data; it is not a promise of zero collection.

Commercial advertising requires verified publisher settings, commercial broadcast rights, production identifiers, configured consent messages, app-ads.txt verification and updated App Store privacy disclosures. It is not approved or enabled by this candidate. Google and Apple control their own review decisions.
