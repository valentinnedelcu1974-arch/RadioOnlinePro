# Radio 43 — Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Updated for version 1.0 Romanian-directory candidate (build 10); build 9 uses only the bundled international catalog. Applies to this candidate, bundle com.radioonlinepro.player. Earlier builds had an optional advertising test; this build removes Google Mobile Ads and UMP entirely.

Developer: Iulian Valentin Nedelcu. Contact: valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com.

## Local data
Station favorites, listening references and personal notes are stored in local iOS preferences. Searches run locally. No developer backend, user accounts, subscriptions, advertising SDKs or analytics are included. No tracking, IDFA access, microphone, camera, contacts or location permission. No audio recording or downloads. Local data is removed on app deletion, subject to Apple backup settings.

## Connections
Playback connects directly to the selected broadcaster: icecast.vrtcdn.be for the 21 VRT streams or radio.mdkband.com for MDK. These providers receive IP address and connection details and may keep their own logs. MDK metadata is requested before playback and every 20 seconds to verify attribution. VRT playback does not request MDK metadata. The Romanian directory is loaded from Radio Browser API servers under api.radio-browser.info on launch and on refresh. Radio Browser receives the IP address, app User-Agent and ordinary connection details; no search text, favorites or notebook notes are sent. The server-discovery endpoint and complete country-code RO list are requested, not user listening history or station click counters. The returned metadata is cached locally and searched locally. Audio connects directly to the chosen station server, which can receive IP/connection details and keep its own logs. The developer has no control over these external providers’ retention policies. Opening source/license links sends ordinary browser data to the destination site.

The developer does not collect these playback requests through a developer server. No app-added ads are shown; broadcaster-originated programming may contain its own advertising.

## Support and Apple
If you email support, the developer receives your email and information you include, used to handle that request. Request deletion at the contact address. Apple handles TestFlight, App Store, backups and optional diagnostics under its own policies. Feedback/crash information you choose to share through Apple may be available to the developer.
