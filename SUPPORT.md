# Radio Online Pro — Support

Contact: **valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com**

Radio Online Pro is a native iPhone player for HTTPS internet radio streams. Version 1.0 supports iOS 17 and later. No app account is required.

## Start listening

Open Posturi VRT, search by name or genre, then tap a station to play it. Tap its heart to save it in Favorites. This release includes only VRT streams published by the broadcaster for third-party apps. It does not include Romanian stations, a public directory or custom stream entry.

## Playback and connection issues

Check Wi-Fi/mobile data, try another station, then use the reconnect button. Some streams are offline, geographically restricted or incompatible. App availability does not guarantee availability of every listed station. Only HTTPS stream addresses are supported.

Use the iPhone volume controls. Lock-screen play/pause, Bluetooth/headset controls and AirPlay depend on your device, audio route and receiver. The Sleep menu stops playback after the selected interval. Audio streaming may incur mobile-data charges.

## Favorites and saved stations

Favorites and station details are saved locally on the device. Swipe a saved station to remove it. Removing the app may remove its saved data. There is no developer-hosted account or cloud synchronization.

## Report a problem

Email the station name, iOS version, app version, network type and a short description. If useful, include a screenshot with personal information removed. Do not send passwords or payment data. Audio delivery is provided by VRT and its delivery services.

## Content and station concerns

The catalog uses VRT's official published stream URLs: https://www.vrt.be/nl/ons-aanbod/streamingslinks-radio . Audio is delivered directly by broadcasters. The app is not affiliated with the listed stations and does not record, download or host broadcasts. Broadcasters and rights holders can contact support regarding a listing or stream.
