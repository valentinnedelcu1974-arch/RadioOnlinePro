# Radio Online Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026. Applies to iOS version 1.0, bundle com.radioonlinepro.player.

Developer: Iulian Valentin Nedelcu. Privacy/support contact: valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com.

## Data kept on your iPhone

The app stores saved station names, stream URLs and favorites in local iOS app preferences. These records are not sent to a developer-operated backend. You can remove saved stations in the app. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to any backup settings you control through Apple.

## Internet connections

The catalog is bundled with the app. Searching and filtering the catalog happen locally and do not send your search text to a directory. The app no longer connects to Radio Browser.

When you play a station, your iPhone connects directly to VRT's official stream service and broadcaster-controlled delivery servers. Those services receive your IP address and the technical information needed to deliver audio. The developer does not host, record or relay audio. VRT determines its own logging, retention and privacy practices. We cannot promise it retains no connection logs. See VRT's privacy information at https://www.vrt.be/nl/info/privacybeleid.

## Accounts, tracking and permissions

Version 1.0 has no account registration, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated user database, purchases or subscriptions. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not record audio. Favorites remain local. The developer does not sell app-user data or use it for advertising tracking.

## Support requests

If you choose to email support, the developer receives the information you include, such as your email address, app/device details and the issue description. Do not send passwords, payment information or other secrets. Support information is used to respond and resolve the issue. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any legal obligation to retain it.

## Apple services

App Store, TestFlight, device backups and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple operate under Apple's policies and your device settings. The developer may receive crash/feedback information that you choose to provide through Apple or support.

## Changes and questions

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. For questions, privacy requests or support, email valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com.
